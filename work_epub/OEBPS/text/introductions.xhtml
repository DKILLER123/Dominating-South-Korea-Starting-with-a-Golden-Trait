First Look

Introductions

The Character Info page keeps the facts. This page keeps the entrance: who arrives first, what they are wearing in the reader’s imagination, and the one line that should make a chapter feel obvious when that person walks in.

Bae Do-yoon

Prosecutor, one week in the chair. Reborn. Still annoyed about it.

Seoul National University · College of Law · a rented one-bedroom he can explain

Entries: gold, purple, purple, blue — and one of them is the reason he was chosen

He wanted a girl-group girlfriend and got a state institution instead. Polite at funerals, blunt with power, and in the habit of writing his own situation down in seven numbered lines before he is allowed to feel it.

Lead

Jessica

Singer and label founder, walking out of a hotel at noon in sunglasses

Formerly Girls’ Generation · flagged a controversial artist by an industry that has not said so aloud

Entered the story through a clause, not a choice

She slams a table, is told the meeting itself is proof she has no options, and negotiates for exactly one fact before she agrees to anything. Proud, tired, and the only person in this book who is used by both sides of a transaction at once.

Chapter 1 · and Chapter 4

Lee Boo-jin

The President. Head of the Shilla; the eldest daughter of the house.

Behind a door with no plaque, at the end of four sealed corridors

Watching him, by her own account, since a welfare-home visit he barely remembers

She does not threaten; she describes weather. A woman with more power than she can freely use, raising an edge of her own inside a state instrument — and amused, rather than offended, when the man she is building tells her he intends to make her pay for it.

The hand

Krystal

Singer and actress; the reason her sister signs nothing

SM · half-shelved, marginalized, still good enough to be a problem for somebody

Said, in a dressing room, that she was fine

Cool face, thin skin, and the bad habit of absorbing a hit so that nobody has to admit it landed. Used in this book twice as a lever and once as a person; the second use is the one that stays.

The soft place

The President’s assistant

Black suit, pinned hair, a polite smile worn like a uniform

No name in the raw through Chapter 5, so none here either

Escorted one man through four sealed doors; sat with one woman over coffee

Never raises her voice, never needs to. She is the part of a very large machine that says we are not a gang in exactly the tone that proves it.

Unnamed

The entries

Annotation lines only one man can read, graded white to red

No quests, no punishment, no voice going ping — only text, and only for him

First one arrived gold, at the start of this life

A cheat that behaves like a clause: every gain printed beside its cost. Favor of Rich Women picked him out; Judicial Monster makes him impossible to fool and worse at feeling; Idol Killer names the room he is already standing in.

System

Boram

T-ara’s eldest-line member, living quietly one door away

Cap low in public, pale blue loungewear at home, hands always clasped in front of her

Moved out of the dormitory for quiet; the group in a valley since 2012

She reported nobody in the end, apologized twice for almost doing it, and closed her own door on the night’s second real laugh. Her offer at his doorstep — knock, I’m right next door, let me help — is the only verdict this book files on her.

The neighbor

Kwon Young-il

The brand’s other half; the call Jessica declined, then audited

Also written Tyler Kwon in the English press; in this book, Kwon Young-il

Known so far only by voice: hurried, aggrieved, excited, cooled

Every channel outlay, investment line, inventory cost and external contract, by noon — or a lawyer collects them. He asked who she had been listening to. She hung up first this time.

The ledger

Kim Mi-ju

Investigative officer assigned to Criminal Division 3 — his officer

Famous-university graduate who once sat the Judicial Examination herself

Heard his name before he badged in; doubted his reading speed to his face

Doubts aloud, verifies fairly with the bottom file, warns without fanning flames or picking sides, pays wager lunches on time, and conceals one pop loyalty so deeply that only a prosecutor’s memory will ever find it.

The officer

Bae Suzy

JYP entertainer, twenty-one; the petition’s protagonist

The Nation’s First Love, as a quoted label only

Comments, then emails, then gifts, then figures at non-public routes

She answers every question she can and watches the man asking them a second too long; at the lobby she folds his business card — and one unfiled feeling — into the same hand.

The petition

Park Jin-young

Founder of JYP Entertainment; representative-nim of every greeting

The man who still tells the SM-audition story on television

Met in a meeting room, mid-case, with his top star behind him

Admits the over-processed material without arguing, offers direct cooperation, and gives a tour of his company the way another man offers an alibi — neatly, and with a reason attached.

The founder

Park Dae-jun

Legal-room lawyer, JYP Entertainment

Great courtesy, a few parts carefulness, thin-framed glasses

First seen at the side-gate visitor entrance, badge printer warming

Accompanies every visit, prepares every bundle, and notes at walking pace that a stranger’s outstanding face will be misread in a building full of trainees.

The escort

Park Jung-hwan

Senior prosecutor who dislikes newcomers that shine

A few years earlier in; ability not poor; patience, poorer

Named over a wager lunch; sighted once across a tray return

Has not spoken yet. Sent materials to be delivered, watched them come back handled flawlessly, and went quiet — for now. The office’s held breath has his name on it.

The minority

Park Ji-yeon

T-ara member; the neighbor’s neighbor, as of one cold night

Recognized behind cap and mask by her eyes alone

First line on record: “Unnie — who is this?”

Counts the kindnesses other people perform without noticing — doors waited at, seats not taken, questions not asked — and reserves, aloud, the right to get to know their author slowly.

The curious one

The Department Chief Prosecutor

Department head; the hand that assigned the Bae Suzy case

Read the young prosecutor’s whole record before the young prosecutor arrived

Met at nightfall, unsurprised, behind a desk of stacked files

Gives hints that stop one word short of orders, orders distance from the famous party, and after the door closes dials a number to say: ability, yes — cleverness, to be seen.

The chief

The Master of Inmudang

Head instructor, Inmudang Chinese Martial Arts Hall

Lineage of a how-many-th generation, per the listing; of the mat, per the comments

Met mid-class on a day off, mid-throw of a meter-eighty student

Teaches force as a ledger — spend it wildly and the other side reads your balance — in Korean with a little Chinese accent in it.

The master

Irene

Leader of Red Velvet; the old friend from before the name

SM Entertainment; trainee years measured in monthly evaluations

A station afternoon: one suitcase, one unlicensed driver, one calm prosecutor

Messages in two lines with no stickers; plans doors and basements before coffee; keeps her reasons until asked twice — and laughs, when finally at ease, as if afraid of being heard outside.

The old friend

Lee Yoon-hyang

Lee Boo-jin’s younger sister; the name that is spoken in a lowered voice

The Lee household, ten years before this book begins

A photograph on a desk corner, gone over a personal feeling, yin and yang apart

Never in a room, never a line of dialogue — and the reason the plan exists: the face her sister lost once, and the face her sister has been watching for in others ever since.

The sister in the frame

Mina

Trainee at JYP; the ten-member group that debuts this year

American-Japanese; usually quiet — a different story if she is running late

The corridor collision: one thud, one fall, one bow before the pain registered

Calls a prosecutor “senior,” and corrects herself a half-beat after the president does; grits her teeth in pain while apologizing first — clumsy, not malicious.

The collision

Taeyeon

Leader of Girls’ Generation; the captain’s captain

SM Entertainment; the elevator door at the end of the workday

A private room, a hat, and an iced americano in the new captain’s hands

Gives counsel four courses at a time and never in a hurry; ends the consultation at the sizzle of the beef, on the meat; says “I’m a little tired” the way other people say “excuse me.”
