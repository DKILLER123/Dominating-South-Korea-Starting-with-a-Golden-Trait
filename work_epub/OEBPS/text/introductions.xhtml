Introductions

The Character Info page keeps the facts. This page keeps the entrance: who arrives first, what they are wearing in the reader’s imagination, and the one line that should make a chapter feel obvious when that person walks in.

Bae Do-yoon

Prosecutor, one week in the chair. Reborn. Still annoyed about it.

Seoul National University · College of Law · a rented one-bedroom he can explain

Entries: gold, purple, purple, blue — and one of them is the reason he was chosen

He wanted a girl-group girlfriend and got a state institution instead. Polite at funerals, blunt with power, and in the habit of writing his own situation down in seven numbered lines before he is allowed to feel it.

Lead

Jessica

Singer and label founder, walking out of a hotel at noon in sunglasses

Formerly Girls’ Generation · flagged a controversial artist by an industry that has not said so aloud

Entered the story through a clause, not a choice

She slams a table, is told the meeting itself is proof she has no options, and negotiates for exactly one fact before she agrees to anything. Proud, tired, and the only person in this book who is used by both sides of a transaction at once.

Chapter 1 · and Chapter 4

Lee Boo-jin

The President. Head of the Shilla; the eldest daughter of the house.

Behind a door with no plaque, at the end of four sealed corridors

Watching him, by her own account, since a welfare-home visit he barely remembers

She does not threaten; she describes weather. A woman with more power than she can freely use, raising an edge of her own inside a state instrument — and amused, rather than offended, when the man she is building tells her he intends to make her pay for it.

The hand

Krystal

Singer and actress; the reason her sister signs nothing

SM · half-shelved, marginalized, still good enough to be a problem for somebody

Said, in a dressing room, that she was fine

Cool face, thin skin, and the bad habit of absorbing a hit so that nobody has to admit it landed. Used in this book twice as a lever and once as a person; the second use is the one that stays.

The soft place

The President’s assistant

Black suit, pinned hair, a polite smile worn like a uniform

No name in the raw through Chapter 5, so none here either

Escorted one man through four sealed doors; sat with one woman over coffee

Never raises her voice, never needs to. She is the part of a very large machine that says we are not a gang in exactly the tone that proves it.

Unnamed

The entries

Annotation lines only one man can read, graded white to red

No quests, no punishment, no voice going ping — only text, and only for him

First one arrived gold, at the start of this life

A cheat that behaves like a clause: every gain printed beside its cost. Favor of Rich Women picked him out; Judicial Monster makes him impossible to fool and worse at feeling; Idol Killer names the room he is already standing in.

System

Cast cards are appended the cycle after a figure is first named in a translated chapter, and the same card vocabulary is used inside the chapters for a character’s first appearance. Nothing on this page may be reused as evidence for a fact a chapter has not yet stated.
