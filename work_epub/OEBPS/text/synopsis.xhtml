The Book

Synopsis

They all say the same thing: get reborn on the Peninsula and give yourself a sweet little romance with an idol.

Bae Do-yoon believed it too. That was the whole plan, laid out the morning he woke up back in his own teenage life: finish high school, walk into SM Entertainment as an artist, find an idol, date her quietly, and live a shamelessly happy life with no one to answer to.

The plan was cut in front of him. Someone stepped into the queue, produced two options, and refused to discuss a third.

Two Options · Non-Negotiable

A reborn life is still a life, and a life with a handle on it is worth more than a life without one.

Feed the fish, or feed the country. The river has no opinion.

He passed the exam. He took the seat. And the day he became a prosecutor, the same stranger delivered Jessica into his bed — Jessica, freshly out of Girls’ Generation, a mess of scandals tracking behind her like spilled perfume.

Only then did Do-yoon finally see the stranger’s face.

Lee Boo-jin made her terms plain. “Work for me, and work well. Become the blade that is mine alone. Money, power, beautiful women. Name what you want and I will put it in your hand.”

Bae Do-yoon’s answer was a pause with nothing in it.

He looked down at the golden trait entry his own life had come with, and he sighed, because a cheat is a cheat, and it had been the cheat’s fault from the beginning.

So Bae Do-yoon climbed. The chaebol houses started seeing wolves and tigers in the shape of a young man in a cheap suit. The Peninsula’s high benches started calling him Yama’s judge, and stopped sleeping well. And once, years on, he stood still long enough to look back and found that the entire Peninsula was already under his feet.
